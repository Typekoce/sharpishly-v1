Know What Comes First

Priority and non-priority debts are not the same

Some missed payments can put your home, utilities or freedom at risk. These normally need to be kept up to date before non-priority debts.

Comparison of priority and non-priority debts
Debt typeClassificationWhy it mattersUsually included in a debt plan?
Mortgage or rent arrearsPriorityYour home could be at risk if arrears are not addressed.Usually kept separate and paid first
Council TaxPriorityLocal authorities can use enforcement action for unpaid arrears.Usually kept separate and paid first
Gas and electricity arrearsPrioritySupplies may be restricted and energy arrears can escalate.Usually kept separate and paid first
Court finesPriorityNon-payment can lead to enforcement action.Usually kept separate and paid first
Credit cardsNon-priorityMissing payments can harm your credit file and increase charges.Often included, subject to suitability
Store cards and cataloguesNon-priorityInterest and charges can make balances grow quickly.Often included, subject to suitability
Payday loansNon-priorityHigh-cost short-term borrowing can create a repayment spiral.Often included, subject to suitability
Personal loans and overdraftsNon-priorityThese unsecured debts may be suitable for consolidation or a repayment plan.Often included, subject to suitability
