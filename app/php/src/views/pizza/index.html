Specialty Extraction Targets

Ideal Filter Window: 18%–22% Extraction Yield at 1.25%–1.45% TDS.

Under-extracted (<18%): Sharp, sour, hollow finish. Try finer grind or hotter water.

Over-extracted (>22%): Dry, astringent, bitter. Coarsen grind or shorten contact time.
