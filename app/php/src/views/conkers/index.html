Safety

COSHH / CLP Safety Data Sheet

Product: Chestnut Saponin Laundry Concentrate. Intended use: domestic and commercial laundry and hard-surface cleaning.

Warning: Causes serious eye irritation. Keep out of reach of children and pets. Avoid contact with eyes. If in eyes, rinse cautiously with water for several minutes; remove contact lenses if present and easy to do.

Composition: aqueous extract of horse chestnut (Aesculus hippocastanum), English ivy leaf extract, soapwort root extract, plant-derived glycerin, citric acid, sodium benzoate.

Handling: Do not ingest. Do not mix with bleach or acidic descalers. Store closed, upright and away from direct sunlight.

Disposal: Rinse containers before kerbside recycling where accepted; refill pouches can be returned through The Conkers closed-loop collection network.
